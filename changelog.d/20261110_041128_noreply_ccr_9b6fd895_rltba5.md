### Added

- `LocalFacts` has two new fields, `min_support` and `n_samples`, so the facts carry the options that they hold under.
- `sample_size_word` gives the word (`large`, `moderate`, `small`) that a layperson reads for the size of the sample of the completions.

### Changed

- **BREAKING:** `Audience` has three new abstract methods: `guarantee`, `support` and `sample`. An audience that subclasses `Audience` itself must add them. A subclass of `Layperson` or `DataScientist` needs no change. `LocalFacts` needs `min_support` and `n_samples` when you build it yourself.
- `describe_ebm` and `describe_graph` tell the LLM which parts of the model the description leaves out: the interaction terms, and the features that `exclude` or `remove_terms` left without a graph. The text of the description then names them. `describe_ebm` logs a `features_skipped` warning for such features.
- The BAML functions `describe_graph_cot`, `summarize_ebm` and `describe_ebm` accept `omitted_terms`, and the last two accept `omitted_features`. Both default to an empty list.

### Fixed

- The local explanation prompts claim a condition only when the facts state it. With `confidence_level=0` they do not say that a flip or a guarantee survives the uncertainty of the model. With `min_support=0` they do not say that rare values were left out. They tell the LLM the size of the sample of the completions, and they limit the robust flip to the changed features.
- The docstring of `ebaml.local.facts` and the guide name the uncertainty model of each claim. A counterfactual moves only the scores of the changed features. The text said that every score moves.
- The docstrings of `Counterfactual` and `compute_local_facts`, the guide and the MCP tool text say that a set is the smallest only when `minimum` is true, and that multiclass classifiers work. The guide showed an error for a multiclass classifier that the code does not raise.
- The docstring of `Prediction.score` says that the class score of a multiclass classifier uses the scores centred over the classes, as the graphs do.
- The guide and the docstring of the completions name the assumptions of the estimate: independent inputs, the training weights of every bin, the fitted scores and a fixed seed. A share can fall when an input becomes known.
