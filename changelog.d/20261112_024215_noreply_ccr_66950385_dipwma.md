### Added

- `EditSession` and the edits `Shift`, `SetScores`, `Align`, `Monotonize`, `Interpolate`, `FitLine`, `Scale`, `ZeroTerm`, `Recenter`, `RemoveTerms` and `RemoveFeature` change the graphs of a fitted binary classifier or regressor, as the tools of GAM Changer do. The edited model keeps its bagged scores and standard deviations in step with the scores, so `predict_with_uncertainty`, `explain_global`, `EBMGraph.from_ebm` and `skops` work on it.
- The selections `Bins`, `Categories`, `ValueInterval`, `WholeGraph`, `MissingBin` and `UnseenBin` name the bins of an edit. An edit reaches the missing bin and the unseen bin too.
- `EditSession` gives a report for each edit (`preview`, `apply`), undo, redo, checkout, a history table with a reason and a review flag for each edit, and a replayable JSON history (`to_json`, `EditSession.replay`).
- `EditSession.save` writes the edited model with `skops.io` and its history next to it. It needs a message, refuses unreviewed edits, and never overwrites a file.
- Several threads can share one session. A call that changes the head takes `expected_revision` and raises `StaleRevisionError` if the head differs.
- The new documentation page "Editing a model" and the notebook `notebooks/model_editing.py`.
